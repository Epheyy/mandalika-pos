## 4. Owner questions

1. **Refund payout on split orders (D-56).** Today one refund pays out in one method (default: cash if the sale had cash, else the largest line), so a full refund of a split sale moves money between methods and can push Tercatat cash negative. Options: (a) keep D-56, add a warning in the refund dialog plus an explanation on the recap; (b) default a full refund to "each part back to its own method" (needs a per-method payout on the refund record: backend + sync change).
2. **Negative opening cash (F20).** Reject below 0 at both POS entry points (and on the server)?
3. **Cash entry (F18).** One whole-rupiah rule for typing and pasting: recommended digits only, separators ignored, never a fraction.
4. **Held-order amount (F17).** Label it "Subtotal", or show the payable estimate after the saved discount?
